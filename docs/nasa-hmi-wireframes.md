# SignalBoost NASA-Style HMI Wireframes

Preview asset: `/wireframes/signalboost-nasa-hmi-wireframes.svg`.

## Marketplace homepage
- Hero: AI-guided search with contextual suggestions and embedded Concierge widget.
- Category cockpit panels: Flights, Hotels, eSIM & Internet, Tours & Activities, Car Rentals, Marketplace.
- Partner vitrina: trusted partner carousel with neon framing.
- Executive strip: 130+ partners plus live campaigns/bookings telemetry.

## SaaS dashboard modules
- Unified sidebar: Promote Business, Reviews, Calendar, Spreadsheets, Outreach, Personal Assistant.
- Main panel: responsive telemetry cards, charts, and Concierge recommendations.
- Reviews panel: star submission, locale flag, sentiment trend chart, moderation queue.
- Pricing: cockpit cards linked to each SaaS route.

## HMI accessibility notes
- All interactive regions require ARIA labels, visible focus states, keyboard-compatible controls, and LTR locale metadata for English, Spanish, Portuguese, Polish, and Russian.
