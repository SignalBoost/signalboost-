Visa & entry starter

This page is the beginning of SignalBoost’s international travel entry layer. It is designed to route users only to official sources once a country flow is fully verified.

Important: visa and entry rules can change. SignalBoost should only publish live country routes after official government or embassy links are verified. Until then, unfinished country flows remain marked as not live.

How this section will work

Official-source only
  • User selects nationality, destination, purpose of travel, and length of stay.
  • SignalBoost checks whether an official verified country flow exists.
  • If verified, user is routed to the official government or embassy application page.
  • If not verified, the route stays hidden or marked as not live yet.
