Official link policy

U.S. searches use U.S. official entry sources. France searches use France’s official visa portal. Official and commercial links remain separate.

This version fixes the U.S./France official-link mismatch, uses the page sides more effectively, tightens oversized boxes, and shifts the design toward a brighter, feed-oriented platform feel.
